## Overview
A two-stage diffusion and GAN-based pipeline for enhancing digitally reconstructed chest radiographs (DRRs), preserving fine-grained clinical details like subtle lung nodules that traditional super-resolution methods miss.

## The Problem: Enhancing DRRs for Clinical Use
Digitally Reconstructed Radiographs (DRRs) offer a scalable solution for creating training data, but suffer from significant quality issues: blur, loss of fine anatomical structures, and projection artifacts. DiffusionXRay enhances these to match real X-ray quality while preserving clinically important details.
