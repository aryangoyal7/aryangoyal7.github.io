## Overview
This thesis addresses two interconnected challenges in waste management: forecasting MSW generation using economic indicators across countries, and estimating waste volumes from single RGB images using monocular depth inference—together forming a closed-loop data-driven framework for waste monitoring.

## Motivation
Effective solid waste management requires knowing how much waste is generated, where it originates, and what it comprises. We build:
1. **Forecasting models** that infer waste generation from stable socioeconomic variables.
2. **Low-cost volume estimation** systems that operate on-site without expensive hardware.
